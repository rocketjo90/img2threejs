# Feature evidence and contextual geometry review

Use this contract for any subject or renderer. Scale the checks to the intended use and the
available images; it does not require mechanical interfaces on organic or single-piece assets.

## Describe what must be built

For each identity-defining feature, record: stable ID, reference authority, observed/inferred/
unknown status, owning part and neighbors, geometric meaning, target and review evidence.
Distinguish through-openings, blind recesses, raised relief, seams and color-only markings.
A dark patch is not evidence that a hole exists. Conversely, a required opening must remain
an opening in opaque Clay; a black primitive or decal cannot substitute for negative space.
Use exposed views when another real component conceals a feature; uncertainty alone is not
a reason to hide geometry. Simplify unsupported internals explicitly.

Keep raw image landmarks separate from fitted profiles and camera/depth assumptions. Assign
authority by feature rather than averaging variants. Use the same annotated reference and
camera protocol through a correction; record camera changes separately from geometry changes.
An image-mask score is not a percentage of 3D accuracy.

## Build relationships, not isolated parts

Derive adjoining pieces from shared profiles, contact surfaces or socket dimensions. Preserve
intentional gaps/steps; check both position and tangent continuity where a smooth seam is intended.
For important interfaces, nominate the two actual surfaces, sample locations, units, tolerance
and expected coverage before judging them. Check multiple points around curved mounts and
edges. Bounding-box overlap, common parenting and zero reported violations do not establish fit.
Clearance, contact, penetration and motion are distinct checks; label untested ones.

Give movable/editable assemblies useful local origins and axes at their interface or intended
pivot. A root-space mesh with an assembly label is not sufficient. Recenter while preserving
world geometry, test a small temporary motion where relevant, restore it and verify the rest
state. Keep cutters and their targets in compatible coordinate frames. Put object dimensions
and dependent relationships in the parameter record rather than scattered constants.

## Review and stop honestly

Inspect Basic and Clay, assembled and relevant exposed views, including close-ups of critical
negative space and interfaces. Classify evidence certainty separately from outcome. A provisional
feature can be ready for user review without being dimensionally verified. Missing or unseen
evidence is not a pass. Separate mesh integrity, visual fidelity, interface checks and destination
qualification. Do not accept a broad feature merely because its relative position is plausible.

State the expected number of relevant checks. Empty coverage is not-applicable with a reason,
or a failure when interfaces were declared; it must never be described as verified fit. Require
errors from Basic, Clay and restoration audits to stop execution. Keep the geometry-first hold
explicit in the handoff even when a pipeline points at its next appearance stage. Elapsed time
does not grant approval to continue.

## Spend effort where it changes the result

Use modest blockout resolution, then feature crops to diagnose a correction, and a complete
fixed-view review set before accepting changed geometry. Cache only if geometry, transforms,
visibility, camera, lighting and material inputs relevant to that evidence are unchanged.
Do not claim caching or partial-stage acceptance if the runner does not implement it.
Reuse generic profile, opening, conforming-mount and origin utilities; fresh reconstructions
still derive their object-specific dimensions from the supplied references. More triangles
cannot repair an incorrect interface or missing opening. Record evaluated/drawn triangles,
authoring/review/repair time and rendering/packaging time separately when measurable. Report
unavailable token or compute measurements as unavailable, rather than estimating savings.
